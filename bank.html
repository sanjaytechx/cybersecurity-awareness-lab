Legitimate Banking vs Phishing

✓ Safer Approach

  • Open your banking app directly
  • Check the exact website domain
  • Ignore unexpected login links
  • Contact the bank using official contact details

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Unknown or unusual domain
  • Urgent account verification
  • Unexpected login request
  • Link received through an untrusted channel
