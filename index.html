UNDERSTAND THE ATTACK

How Online Scams Usually Work

Many online scams follow a similar social engineering pattern designed to influence the victim's decision.

01

Create Trust

The attacker may imitate a bank, company, friend, delivery service or popular platform.

02

Create Urgency

The message may claim that an account, reward or opportunity requires immediate action.

03

Trigger an Action

The victim may be encouraged to click a link, scan a QR code, install an application or share information.

04

Exploit the Response

The attacker attempts to take advantage of the victim's action or information.

QUICK CHECK

Common Red Flags

Before clicking, scanning, installing or responding, look for these warning signs.

⚠️ Unexpected message or request
🔗 Suspicious or unfamiliar domain
⏱️ Strong urgency or pressure
🔐 Request for sensitive information
📱 Unknown application or APK
🎁 Unexpected prize or reward
💰 Guaranteed or unrealistic financial claims
👤 Suspicious or impersonated account
STAY PROTECTED

How to Stay Safe

Simple habits can help you recognize suspicious requests before taking action.

🔎 Verify

Verify unexpected requests through an independent and trusted source.

🔗 Check Links

Inspect the domain carefully before opening unexpected links.

🔐 Protect Codes

Never share OTPs, verification codes or authentication information with another person.

📱 Use Trusted Apps

Install applications and updates through trusted official sources.

⏸️ Slow Down

Urgency is often used to make people act before they have time to verify.

🚨 Report

Report suspicious messages, accounts and websites through the appropriate platform or authority.
