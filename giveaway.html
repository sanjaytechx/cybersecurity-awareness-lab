Legitimate Giveaway vs Suspicious Giveaway

✓ Verify First

  • Check the organizer
  • Find official giveaway rules
  • Verify through official channels
  • Never pay unexpected fees

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • You never entered the giveaway
  • Very short claim deadline
  • Requests for personal information
  • Unexpected payment or delivery fees
