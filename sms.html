SMS PHISHING / SMISHING

Fake SMS Phishing

Learn how attackers use text messages, urgency, fake links and unexpected alerts to trick people into taking unsafe actions.

verify-account.example

IDENTIFY THE WARNING SIGNS

What Makes This SMS Suspicious?

01

⏱️ Urgency

The message tries to make the recipient act immediately instead of taking time to verify the request.

02

🔗 Suspicious Link

The message directs the recipient to an unfamiliar domain rather than a verified official website.

03

⚠️ Unexpected Alert

If you were not expecting the notification, verify it independently before taking action.

04

🎯 Action Request

The message attempts to push the recipient toward clicking a link before verifying the request.

SAFE VERIFICATION

How Should You Verify the Message?

📱 Use the Official App

Open the organization's official application directly and check whether the notification actually exists.

🌐 Enter the Website Manually

Instead of using the SMS link, manually enter the known official website address.

☎️ Contact the Organization

Use a trusted phone number or contact method obtained independently from the suspicious message.

🗑️ Don't Engage

If the message appears suspicious, avoid clicking links or replying until it has been verified.

QUICK COMPARISON

Safer Response vs. Risky Response

✓ Safer Response

Ignore the link and independently verify the notification through the organization's official app, website or trusted contact method.

⚠️ Risky Response

Immediately clicking the link because the message claims that an account will be restricted.

QUICK CHECK

Common Smishing Red Flags

⚠️ Unexpected SMS
⏱️ Urgent deadline
🔗 Unknown or suspicious domain
🔐 Request for sensitive information
💳 Unexpected payment request
🎁 Unexpected reward or prize
👤 Unknown sender
❗ Threat of account suspension
KEY TAKEAWAY

Stop. Verify. Then Act.

A text message can look convincing without being legitimate. Check the sender, inspect the destination and independently verify unexpected requests.
