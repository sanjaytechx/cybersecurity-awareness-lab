Genuine Promotion vs Reward Scam

✓ Safer Approach

  • Verify the promotion independently
  • Check the official website
  • Confirm the sender
  • Avoid unexpected links

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Unexpected reward
  • Large monetary amount
  • Short claim deadline
  • Unknown website
